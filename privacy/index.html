Privacy Policy

RVN Auto Publisher TikTok

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Overview

RVN Auto Publisher TikTok is a Windows desktop application intended to help users manage and publish videos to their authorized TikTok accounts.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the permissions granted, the application may process TikTok account identifiers, authorization tokens, selected video files, captions, and publishing schedules.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare uploads, submit videos through supported TikTok APIs, and display publishing results or errors.

4. Local Storage

The application is designed to operate on the user's Windows computer. Its intended design is to store application settings and authorization information locally. Actual storage and security controls will depend on the released implementation.

5. Sharing With TikTok

Authorized video content and necessary account information may be transmitted to TikTok to perform requested publishing operations. TikTok processes information according to its own privacy policies.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request disconnection of their TikTok account and deletion of locally stored application data. The specific deletion procedure will be documented with the released application.

7. Security

The application is intended to use official authorization methods and secure network connections. No software can guarantee absolute security.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as application features and data practices change.

9. Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests should be directed to the application's operator through its published support contact channel.
